Elevated Technology

Source: MOFCOM / State Council · Jan 29, 2025
Canada Impact
Canadian semiconductor supply chain exposed; critical minerals strategy acceleration likely. Potential opportunity for Canadian rare earth mining sector.
What to Watch
Allied response and coordination on alternative supply chains; Canadian Critical Minerals Strategy update expected Q2.
