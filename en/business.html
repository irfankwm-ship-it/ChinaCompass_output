Market Signals

China Extends Anti-Dumping Tariffs on Canadian Lumber

Elevated Trade
MOFCOM announced a five-year extension of anti-dumping duties on Canadian softwood lumber imports, maintaining tariffs of 8.5–22.1% on major producers.
Source: MOFCOM Notice #12-2025 · Jan 29, 2025

China Restricts Rare Earth Exports Citing National Security

Elevated Technology
New export controls on gallium, germanium, and other critical minerals take effect immediately, citing national security concerns. Industry analysts expect supply chain disruptions across semiconductor and defense sectors.
Source: MOFCOM · Jan 28, 2025

PBOC Cuts Reserve Requirement Ratio by 50 Basis Points

Moderate Economic
The People's Bank of China reduced the reserve requirement ratio by 50 basis points, releasing approximately ¥1.2 trillion in liquidity. The move signals continued efforts to stimulate economic growth amid slowing domestic demand.
Source: PBOC · Jan 27, 2025

SAMR Launches Antitrust Review of Cloud Computing Sector

Elevated Legal
The State Administration for Market Regulation has initiated an antitrust investigation targeting Alibaba Cloud, Tencent Cloud, and Huawei Cloud market practices. The review focuses on pricing strategies, data portability, and market dominance.
Source: SAMR · Jan 26, 2025

CAC Issues New Rules on AI-Generated Content

Moderate Technology
The Cyberspace Administration of China has published new regulations requiring registration for all generative AI services operating within China. The rules mandate content labeling, algorithm transparency, and user identity verification, effective March 2025.
Source: CAC · Jan 25, 2025
